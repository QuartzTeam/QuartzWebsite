# Judgement

A row of hit-judgement counts — Overload through Perfect to Miss — shown as an overlay during gameplay.

## Where to find it

Open the Quartz menu in-game and pick **Overlay → Judgement** from the sidebar.

## Highlights

- **Show XPerfect** — splits the Perfect count into early / X / late instead of one combined number. Works with the XPerfect mod, or on an ADOFAI version with X-Perfect built in (the r150 alpha and later) whenever the game's Perfect text setting shows X-Perfect grades — there the X count also takes the game's X-Perfect colour with a gradient outline. Hidden unless X-Perfect is available.
- **Size** and **Spacing** control the whole row; **Text Shadow** adds an offset, soft-edged shadow with its own color.

## Hiding the game's own judgement popups

That is a separate feature with its own install: [Hide Judgements](hide-judgements.md), on the **Visuals** tab. It suppresses **A Dance of Fire and Ice**'s per-hit popups grade by grade, and works whether or not you use this overlay.
